## Customer Content Data

_Customer content data is content which a Ubicloud customer or any end user transfers to us when using Ubicloud Services for processing, storage or hosting in connection with a customer's Ubicloud account, and any computational results that a customer or their end user derives from the foregoing through their use of Ubicloud services. Our customers maintain full ownership and control of this data and we are generally unable to determine if a customer is processing personal data as part of its customer content data. See our [Terms of Service](/about/terms-of-service). If you are a Customer resident in the EEA that is using a Ubicloud managed service and any of your or your End User's Customer Content Data includes personal data, please visit our [trust center](/security/trust-center) to review and sign our data processing agreement._

### Ubicloud Managed Runners for GitHub Actions

For the GitHub Actions service, Ubicloud runners host data within Europe by default. Workloads may fall back to the USA only if explicitly enabled at the customer's request. If you need to enable or disable specific regions for Ubicloud runners, please get in touch with our team at [support@ubicloud.com](mailto:support@ubicloud.com).

| Name of Sub-Processor | Nature of Processing Activity                  | Location of Processing | Ubicloud Region |
|-----------------------|------------------------------------------------|------------------------|-----------------|

| Leaseweb Germany      | Infrastructure (server) and data hosting       | Germany                | EU              |
| Latitude.sh           | Infrastructure (server) and data hosting       | Germany                | EU              |
| AWS                   | Infrastructure (server) and data hosting\*     | EU                     | EU              |

\* At times of high demand we may fall back to AWS or Google Cloud to prevent long queue times.

\*\* Your workloads may fall back to the US only if explicitly enabled at your request.
